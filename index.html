Questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The ready tools run entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib library, so your documents are not sent to any server.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Encrypted files are rejected with a clear message. Protect and Unlock are not built yet.

Why are some tools unavailable?

Compression, Word conversion, PDF to JPG and similar tools need extra engines or server processing. They stay disabled until they work.
